The Problem

A comprehension crisis is costing plans billions

Documents nobody can read

  • Plan documents (EOC, SB, ANOC) are written at an ~11th–13th-grade level.
  • The average U.S. adult reads at ~8th grade; most seniors prefer ≤6th.
  • Beneficiaries can't tell what's actually covered.

Choice overload

  • A typical county offers 40+ Medicare Advantage plans.
  • Overwhelmed beneficiaries pick on price alone — leaving benefits on the table.
  • Poor fit drives rapid disenrollment and churn.

Expensive confusion

  • Millions of repetitive "is this covered / how do I" calls a year.
  • Live agent contacts are the costliest service channel.
  • Confusion erodes CAHPS scores that drive Star bonuses.
Every avoidable call, every wrong-plan pick, every disenrollment is margin destruction for the plan.

The Solution

Two capabilities, grounded in the member's real plan

1 · Plain-language translation

  • Connect any plan document — EOC, Summary of Benefits, ANOC, formulary.
  • Rendered at a ≤6th-grade reading level, in the beneficiary's language.
  • Turns 12th-grade legalese into a clear, scannable answer.
  • Drives Section 508 / accessibility compliance automatically.

2 · Real-time conversational AI

  • Ask anything by text or voice — coverage, costs, networks, drugs, enrollment.
  • Grounded in the member's actual plan, with citations to the source.
  • Multilingual; serves low-vision & limited-English beneficiaries.
  • We don't hallucinate benefits — every answer is traceable.
