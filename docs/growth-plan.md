# Growth Plan — Ringback (First 30 Days)

Owner: Growth Agent. Reviewed by Sales, Analytics, and Legal Agents.

The 30-day goal is **5 paid pilots at Tier-1 ($249/mo) or higher** by D30.

We earn the right to scale paid acquisition only after pilots prove unit
economics. Until then, every channel is *manual, owner-operator-grade
outbound* with strict compliance and quality bars.

---

## 1. Offer Stack

The hero offer:

> **"Stop losing jobs to missed calls. We answer, qualify, and book — voice
> and text — for $249/mo + $25 per booked job. No contracts. Live in 24
> hours. 14 days free."**

Reasons-to-believe:
- "Built for HVAC, plumbing, roofing, and electrical." (vertical fit)
- "Pays for itself with one recovered job." (ROI math)
- "Inbound only. We never cold-call your customers." (compliance posture)
- "Books straight into your Google Calendar / Jobber / HCP via Zapier."
- "Hear a real recovered call in 90 seconds." (proof CTA)

Risk-reversers:
- 14-day free trial, no card.
- Month-to-month.
- Money-back guarantee on the *subscription* portion of month 1.
  (We never refund per-booking fees because the bookings really happened.)

## 2. Positioning

Against Numa, Goodcall, Smith.ai, GoHighLevel: Ringback is the
trade-priced, true-booking, inbound-only AI receptionist for U.S.
home-services SMBs. We are not enterprise. We are not a CRM. We are
not an outbound dialer.

Tagline candidates (A/B in week 1):
1. "We pick up when your crew can't."
2. "Stop losing jobs to missed calls."
3. "An AI receptionist priced like an HVAC tool, not enterprise software."

## 3. Channels (ordered by speed-to-revenue)

### Channel 1 — Manual outbound (cold email + LinkedIn DM)

- 100 cold emails / week, 30 LinkedIn DMs / week, per founder.
- Audience built from public state license boards + Google Maps owner pages.
- Sender warmed via Mailreef / Smartlead-equivalent for ≥ 14 days.
- Strict throttling (≤ 50 / inbox / day, ≤ 1 inbox / domain initially).
- Reply rate target: ≥ 5%; positive-reply rate target: ≥ 1.5%.
- A/B test pain-led vs. curiosity-led subject lines (see `validation-plan.md`).

### Channel 2 — Trade Facebook groups + Reddit

- 5 value-first posts/week. Examples:
  - "Here's the actual math on a missed-call rate of 35% for a 3-truck shop."
  - "Walkthrough: how we wired up an after-hours AI receptionist for under $300/mo."
- DMs only when an owner reacts or asks. Never unsolicited DMs to group members.

### Channel 3 — Trade-association partner play

- Outreach to: 1 regional Plumbing-Heating-Cooling Contractors (PHCC)
  chapter, 1 ACCA chapter, 1 Air Conditioning Contractors of America
  affiliate, 1 NACHI inspector group.
- Pitch: free pilot for 3 of their members in exchange for a member
  newsletter feature once pilot results are in.

### Channel 4 — Reseller / consultant referrals

- One-page partner sheet for Jobber/HCP/ServiceTitan-implementation
  consultants. Rev-share: 20% recurring for 12 months on referrals.
- We do NOT pay non-disclosed kickbacks to any company employee. (Legal.)

### Channel 5 — Paid (gated; only after first 5 paying customers)

- Google Local Services Ads.
- Meta retargeting only.
- We never run paid acquisition on cold audiences until product retention
  is proven.

## 4. Lead Magnets

## 7. Brand & Voice Guidelines

- Direct, plain-spoken. We don't say "leverage" or "synergize."
- We sound like a contractor who happens to know AI, not a SaaS bro.
- Always reference real dollars and real calls; avoid vague "10x" claims.
- Never imply we are anything but inbound-only.

## 8. Compliance Guardrails (Legal Agent confirmed)

- No purchased lead lists. No scraping any site that prohibits it via ToS.
- Cold email: every send includes physical address + 1-click unsubscribe.
  CAN-SPAM compliant.
- LinkedIn DMs: respect opt-outs in the same thread; suppression list
  shared between channels.
- SMS: only on explicit opt-in (form fill or reply "YES"). Stop / Help
  keywords honored. 10DLC registration required for production sends.
- Two-party-consent recording disclosure on every voice agent open.
- We never make claims about specific revenue without a real case study.
