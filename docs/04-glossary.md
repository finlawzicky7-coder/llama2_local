# Glossary

**AEP** — Annual Enrollment Period. Oct 15 – Dec 7 each year. Most Medicare enrollment volume happens here.

**ATDS** — Automatic Telephone Dialing System. Subject to TCPA prior-express-written-consent.

**CMS** — Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services. The federal agency overseeing Medicare.

**DNC** — Do Not Call. Federal registry plus state-level registries.

**FCC** — Federal Communications Commission. Issues TCPA implementation rules.

**FMO** — Field Marketing Organization. Upline aggregator that contracts with carriers and supports independent agencies/agents.

**HIPAA** — Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. Privacy and security expectations for PHI.

**IEP** — Initial Enrollment Period. The 7-month window around an individual's 65th birthday during which they can first enroll in Medicare.

**LOA** — Line of Authority. The product line your state license covers (Health, Life, etc.).

**MA / MAPD** — Medicare Advantage / Medicare Advantage with Prescription Drug Coverage (Part C with Part D bundled).

**MBI** — Medicare Beneficiary Identifier. The 11-character identifier on a beneficiary's Medicare card. **Do not collect at lead intake.**

**MCMG** — Medicare Communications and Marketing Guidelines. CMS's authoritative guidance on what TPMOs and plans can say, when, and how.

**MS / Medigap** — Medicare Supplement plan. Sold separately from Medicare Part D.

**NPN** — National Producer Number. Unique identifier per agent across states.

**OEP** — Open Enrollment Period (Medicare Advantage). Jan 1 – Mar 31; one switch allowed.

**PDP** — (Standalone) Prescription Drug Plan. Part D alone.

**PEWC** — Prior Express Written Consent. The TCPA standard for telemarketing calls/texts to wireless numbers.

**PHI** — Protected Health Information.

**RLS** — Row Level Security. Postgres feature; enforced in Supabase to gate per-tenant / per-role data access.

**SEP** — Special Enrollment Period. Triggered by life events (move, loss of coverage, etc.).

**SHIP** — State Health Insurance Assistance Program. Free counseling for beneficiaries; we point leads to it as part of the disclaimer.

**SOA** — Scope of Appointment. CMS-required form documenting the products to be discussed; must be signed before plan-specific conversation.

**STIR/SHAKEN** — Caller-ID authentication standard required by the TRACED Act.

**SU/T65** — Turning 65 / "T65."

**TCPA** — Telephone Consumer Protection Act.

**TPMO** — Third-Party Marketing Organization. Per CMS's MCMG, TPMOs include any organization that conducts marketing on behalf of an MA / Part D plan, sponsor, or another TPMO. **You are likely a TPMO.**

**TSR** — Telemarketing Sales Rule. FTC's parallel set of marketing rules.
