# Phase 7 — Traffic Generation

Three channels per funnel. Ranked by speed to first lead.

## MTR (Travel Nurses)

### 1. Furnished Finder listings (same-day first leads)
- Create operator account at `furnishedfinder.com`.
- List every unit with the EXACT phrasing used on the landing page
  (monthly rate, hospitals nearby, pet policy, stipend notes).
- Put the landing page URL in the listing description as "Full availability →".
- Expected: 2–5 inbound inquiries per unit per week during contract season
  (Jan–Apr, Jul–Sep).

### 2. Direct outreach to travel-nurse Facebook groups
- Groups: "Travel Nursing", "Travel Nurse Housing", "Travel Nurses Northern California".
- Post weekly availability summary, link to landing page. NO spam — share
  value (e.g. "Sutter parking is free after 6pm") then mention availability.
- Track with `?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=<group>`.

### 3. SEO content (compounds over 30–90 days)
Target these long-tail phrases:
- "furnished housing near UC Davis Medical Center"
- "travel nurse housing Sacramento stipend"
- "monthly rentals Kaiser Roseville"
- "furnished 1 bedroom Sutter Medical Center"

Build a `/blog/` subdirectory on the MTR site with one post per phrase, each
linking to the `#apply` form.

## Cleaning / Turnover

### 1. Google Business Profile (same day)
- Create GBP for service area business: Sacramento, Rocklin, Roseville, Elk Grove.
- Categories: "Cleaning service", "House cleaning service".
- Post weekly updates with before/after photos of real turnovers.

### 2. Direct outreach to hosts (highest-ROI hour you'll spend)
- Pull Sacramento + Rocklin Airbnb listings, extract host names.
- Cold DM / email template in `docs/outreach-templates.md` (below).
- Target: 50 hosts contacted → 5 replies → 2 bookings. Repeat weekly.

### 3. Partnership with property managers
- 10 local PMs in Sacramento manage 20–100 units each. Pitch flat-rate
  turnover as a subcontractor. One PM deal = 20+ monthly turns.

## Shared: Review flywheel

Every booked service → ask for a Google review after 48 hours (n8n:
`followup_review`). First 10 5-star reviews typically double inbound volume
on GBP.

## Traffic budget guardrails

- No paid ads until organic leads ≥ 5/day per funnel. Paid ads on cold
  funnels burn cash faster than they teach you anything.
- First paid test: Meta ads targeting "travel nurses" interest, $15/day for
  7 days, creative = static photo of a real unit + stipend callout.
