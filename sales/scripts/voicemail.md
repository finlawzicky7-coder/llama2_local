# Voicemail Script — Outbound

Keep it under 25 seconds. Compliance > clever.

---

## V1 — Speed-to-lead first attempt

> "Hi {first_name}, this is {agent_first_name} with {AGENCY_NAME}, returning your request for help with Medicare. Just so you know, I'm not Medicare or the federal government — we're an independent agency, and we don't offer every plan in your area. You can also reach Medicare.gov or 1-800-MEDICARE for all your options. I'll text you my number and try you again in a bit. To stop hearing from us anytime, just reply STOP to any of our messages. Thanks — talk soon."

## V2 — Day-1 follow-up

> "Hi {first_name}, {agent_first_name} with {AGENCY_NAME} again. Just following up on the Medicare review you asked about — happy to walk through your situation when it's convenient. Same disclosure as before: not Medicare, not the government, multiple carriers represented in your area. Easiest is to text me back at this number. Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Take care."

## V3 — Last call (day 14)

> "Hi {first_name}, {agent_first_name} from {AGENCY_NAME} — just one last note. You'd asked about Medicare options, and I want to respect your time. I'll close out your file at the end of the week unless I hear from you. We're not Medicare or the government — happy to point you to Medicare.gov or your local SHIP if you'd rather go there. Reply STOP to opt out or call me back at this number. All the best."

---

## Required components every voicemail

1. Your first name + agency name.
2. "Not Medicare / not the government."
3. The opt-out instruction.

## Never put in a voicemail

- A specific plan or carrier name.
- A specific benefit, premium, or savings claim.
- A "limited time" or "today only" pressure phrase.
- The lead's full name (treat the device as potentially shared).
