The 7-month Initial Enrollment Period

Your Initial Enrollment Period (IEP) starts 3 months before the month you turn 65, includes your birthday month, and ends 3 months after. Missing this window can lead to penalties on Part B and Part D.

A licensed agent can walk you through:

  • How Original Medicare (Parts A & B) works.
  • The difference between Medicare Advantage and Medicare Supplement (Medigap), in plain English.
  • How prescription drug coverage (Part D) is added.
  • What to do if you're still working and have employer coverage.
